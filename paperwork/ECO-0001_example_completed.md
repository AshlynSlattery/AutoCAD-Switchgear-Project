## Description of change

Initial release of drawing D-201, Medium-Voltage Switchgear Lineup,
One-Line Diagram, at Revision A. The drawing shows the utility source,
main breaker 52-M, the 2000 A main bus, feeder breakers FDR-01 and
FDR-02, transformer T1, and the connected loads.

## Reason for change

The project needs a controlled one-line diagram as the basis for the
rest of the drawing package (elevation, control schematics, bill of
materials). Until this release there was no controlled drawing of the
lineup.
